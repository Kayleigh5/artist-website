Bio

Kayleigh Beard is an electro one-woman act from Groningen, who released her debut album Ocean's Pulse in 2020. Her music combines dreamy beats with high spacious vocals. Kayleigh is inspired by artists such as Eefje de Visser, London Grammar, and Susanne Sundfør. Themes of nature and introspection play a central role in her music, inviting listeners to escape the daily turmoil and feel more connected to life. Kayleigh is known for her mystical music style and energetic live performances.
